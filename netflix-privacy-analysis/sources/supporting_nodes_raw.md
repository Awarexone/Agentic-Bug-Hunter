## node/100624 — What personal information Netflix holds about you and how to request a copy
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/100624

The Account page gives you access to information such as:
- Account information (email, phone number under Security; plan selection under Membership)
- Notification settings (per profile)
- Privacy and Data Settings: Matched Identifier Communications (per profile) and Behavioral Advertising (per profile, except Kids profiles — Netflix does not engage in Behavioral Advertising on Kids profiles)
- Payment and Billing Information (under Membership)
- Profiles (details of any profiles, playback preferences)
- Content Interaction History (Viewing activity per profile)
- Access & device information: shows signed-in devices active on the account in the last 90 days (help.netflix.com/en/node/129831)

To obtain a copy of your data (account owner): go to https://www.netflix.com/account/getmyinfo and follow instructions. May take up to 30 days from the date of verification.
Profile user with email added to a profile: contact privacy@netflix.com; account owner will be notified of the request; up to 30 days to fulfil after identity verification.

## node/100625 — Deletion, removal and retention of information
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/100625

- Account deletion: see help.netflix.com/en/node/126558 (Deleting your Netflix account)
- Account profiles: see help.netflix.com/en/node/10421
- Viewing history: see help.netflix.com/en/node/22205 (How to hide titles from viewing history)
- Payment methods: see help.netflix.com/en/node/113408
- Phone number: see help.netflix.com/en/node/25970
- Email address: required on the account; contact privacy@netflix.com if an email is on an account you didn't create and you want it removed; for secondary-profile email deletion see node/10421.

Retention of Personal Information: "We retain personal information as required or permitted by applicable laws and regulations, including to honor your choices, for our billing or records purposes, and as otherwise necessary to fulfill the purposes described in the Netflix Privacy Statement." (No fixed numeric retention period is stated for general categories.)

## node/124925 — What is a Netflix Household?
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/124925

"When you sign in to Netflix from any device on the same internet connection as your TV, we will automatically establish your Netflix Household based on IP address, device IDs, and account activity. We do not collect GPS data to try to determine the precise physical location of your devices."
You can update your Netflix Household from a TV (node/128339), or check for unrecognized/unwanted devices via netflix.com/manageaccountaccess and select "Sign out of All Devices."

## node/22205 — How to hide titles from viewing history
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/22205

Hiding a title means: it won't show as watched; it won't be used for recommendations unless watched again; it's removed from Continue Watching.
Steps: Account page > Profiles > choose profile > Viewing activity > click the hide icon next to a title (or "Hide all" at bottom to hide all viewing history). Can take up to 24 hours to propagate to all devices. Titles cannot be hidden from a Kids profile's Viewing activity page.

## node/100639 — How Netflix's Recommendations System Works
Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/100639

Recommendations are based on: your interactions with the service (viewing history, ratings), other members with similar tastes, and title metadata (genre, cast, release year). Also factored: time of day, preferred languages, devices used, how long you watched a title. The recommendations system explicitly "does not include demographic information (such as age or gender) as part of the decision making process." New accounts/profiles can optionally pick a few liked titles to "jump start" recommendations (optional step); ongoing engagement supersedes initial picks.
